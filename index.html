ABOUT SHREE MAHALAXMI TEXTILE

The Powerhouse of Petticoat Manufacturing in Bhiwandi.

Located in the heart of Maharashtra’s premier textile capital Bhiwandi, Shree Mahalaxmi Textile (SMT) is a specialized manufacturing & wholesale firm dedicated to high-volume, premium petticoat production.

Under passionate leadership, SMT has earned trust across India through unwavering consistency in yarn selection, exact fabric sizing, accurate cuts (6-kali, 4-kali, shapewear petticoats), and an unmatched library of vibrant color shades. We serve large wholesale markets in Mumbai, Surat, Delhi, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, and beyond.
